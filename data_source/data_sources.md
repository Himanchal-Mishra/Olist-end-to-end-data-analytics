## Dataset Source

Dataset: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset

Source:


Olist E-Commerce Dataset
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

Contains orders, customers, sellers, payments, products, and reviews.

Olist Marketing Funnel Dataset
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/marketing-funnel-olist

Contains marketing qualified leads (MQLs) and closed deals representing seller acquisition via marketing channels.


Provider:
Olist

Description:
The dataset contains transactional e-commerce data including customers, orders, products, payments, reviews, sellers, and logistics information. Additional marketing datasets provide lead acquisition and conversion funnel data.
